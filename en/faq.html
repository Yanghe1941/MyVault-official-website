Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored 100% locally on your iPhone. MyVault has no backend server — your asset records, photos, and stories are never uploaded to the developer or any third party.

What if I switch phones or lose my data?

MyVault supports backup and restore through your personal iCloud. Backups live only in your own iCloud space and are inaccessible to the developer; simply restore on your new device.

Do I need to create an account?

No. MyVault requires no account registration — download and use. It never collects your phone number or email.

Can I export my data?

Yes. MyVault is committed to data export freedom — you can export your full asset data anytime. Your data always belongs to you, with no platform lock-in.

Which platforms are supported?

Currently iOS. Download the release version on the App Store, or join the beta via TestFlight.

How does MyVault protect my privacy?

Privacy-first is MyVault's core design principle: local-first architecture, zero server dependency, and no behavioral tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.

More questions?

Check the User Guide for full feature documentation, or reach us via Settings → Feedback in the app.
